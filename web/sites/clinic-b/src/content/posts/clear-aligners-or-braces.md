Straightening teeth is a big decision, and the choice between clear aligners and traditional braces is often the first one. Neither is better in every case. The right option depends on your teeth, your bite, your age and how you live.

## How each option works

Clear aligners are a series of removable plastic trays. You switch to a new tray as your teeth move, and you take them out to eat and clean your teeth. Traditional braces are brackets bonded to the teeth and joined by a wire, and they stay on for the whole treatment.

## Questions to ask at your consultation

1. What is wrong with my bite, and can this option correct it?
2. How many hours a day will I need to wear aligners, and what happens if I forget?
3. How long will treatment take, and how often will I be seen?
4. What will I wear afterward to hold the result?
5. What happens to the plan if my teeth do not move as expected?

No dentist can promise a result or an exact finish date. Habits such as skipping wear time, along with gum health and age, all play a part.
