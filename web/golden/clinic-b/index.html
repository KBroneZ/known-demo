Our services

Family checkups and cleanings

Exams and cleanings for every age in one visit, with X-rays only when your dentist needs them.

Clear aligners

Removable clear trays that move teeth gradually, planned after an exam and a conversation about your goals.

Children's dentistry

Gentle, unhurried visits for children, starting with a first appointment where the main goal is to feel at home.

Same-day emergency care

Pain, a broken tooth or a lost crown? We hold time each day for emergency visits and an on-call dentist outside office hours.

Quick answers

What are the hours at Copper Wren Dental Studio?
We are open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m., Wednesday from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., and on Saturday from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Outside office hours, leave a message on the main line and the on-call dentist will call you back.
Where is the office and how do I book?
We are at 4107 Shoal Terrace Lane, Suite 3, Austin, TX 78702. Call (512) 555-0176 or email hello@copperwrendental.example to book.
Which dental insurance plans do you accept?
We are in network with Aetna, Guardian and Principal. Our front desk can check your benefits before you come in.
