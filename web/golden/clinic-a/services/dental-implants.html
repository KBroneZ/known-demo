Dental implants

A post placed in the jawbone replaces a missing tooth root and holds a crown. We start with an exam to see whether it is right for you.

A dental implant is a small post placed in the jawbone that acts as an artificial root. After the area heals, a crown is attached to the post.

Whether an implant or a bridge suits you depends on your bone, your neighboring teeth and your health. Your dentist will review these with you at a consultation.

We do not publish implant prices, because the right figure depends on an exam. Our front desk can check your benefits with Delta Dental, Cigna or MetLife.

Led by Dr. Elena Marsh.

Common questions

Is an implant always better than a bridge?

No. It depends on your bone, your neighboring teeth and your health, which your dentist will review with you.

Does getting an implant hurt?

Local anesthetic is used during the procedure, and most patients describe soreness afterward rather than sharp pain. Your dentist will explain what to expect and how to manage it.
